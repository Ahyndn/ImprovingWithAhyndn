# Chapter 12: My Positive Hustling Upswing

## My Successful Promotion
With my new work style, soon my consistently strong workplace performance returned to me. Surprisingly, an area manager recommended me for promotion and my skills needed to reach perfection was my promotion deciding factor; which was notified to me by the store manager. The store manager, leaders, and visual merchandisers were very happy for me. Once I completed my promotion, I had area manager and store manager confirmation that it was successful. My successful promotion was extremely welcomed by all leaders and visual merchandisers, where I had heard waves of congratulations.

## Different Workplace #1: I Am On A Team Again
Not long after my promotion, I was off traveling to work with a few neutral staff for another workplace temporarily and at this workplace was my happiest work memory. Aside from being heavily welcomed by the staff, I no longer always had to work independently at the workplace for a shift. The management staff, was jaw-dropping fantastic when we worked together, had taught me stock management, enhanced my replenishment skills and further taught me much more about inventory analysis. I had not worked with a complete team for a very long time, and I left having met a lot of great people, the neutral staff that worked with me was no longer neutral rather I was now good with them.

## I Helped Prevent Bad Future Possibilities At The Workplace
I returned to my workplace where I was saddened again because I was back to mostly independent work; however, that was not my concern because the workplace had become very difficult for all staff. After my promotion, I was never a leader at my workplace. In my career at this company, I had never led once.

I had enough work experience where I have been taught stock management, inventory analysis, retail sales analysis, cashier, and customer service from management staff. Merchandising strategies, replenishment was taught to me by visual merchandisers and a management staff. Therefore with my work understanding, I decided that I had to discuss with area management because I had saw the revelation of the previously publicly described analysis of a terrifying workplace by the COO to the store manager.

This discussion that started between me with this area manager changed my workplace; area manager immediately brought management into the discussion and then, took action. This was agreed as an emergency by all of management and staff members.

## Different Workplace #2: I Am On Another Team Again
The best part since my promotion was working with different teams and the traveling itself. I got to see different sights along the way to the workplace and I liked not having to work shifts almost always alone. Me with the staff on the team had great workplace dynamics, I got to learn a lot from a sharp-witted store manager about inventory layout and I got to fully apply everything I learned from all the previous management staff; all the visual merchandisers that worked and had still worked together with me.
